### Highlights

- **Reference Model identity:** unified architecture/guide framing across `README.md`, `GETTING-STARTED.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `CITATION.cff`, `mkdocs.yml`, chapters, and `promotion/`, with `Operational & scope notes` replacing `Author practical guidance`
- **Machine-readable Evidence Pack (`schemas/`):** Draft 2020-12 JSON Schema (`CycloneDX 1.6`/`1.7`, `allOf` conditional constraints for `Managed-API` vs self-hosted/`Agentic-MCP`, `dataProvenance`, `localRunnerConfig`, `runtimeTelemetry`) + `Agentic-MCP` and `Managed-API` examples
- **Executable OPA/Conftest policies (`policies/`):** fail-closed admission rules across `CP4`/`CP7`/`CP8`/`CP9` (`package main`), unit tests (`conftest verify`), and Kubernetes inference Pod/Deployment hardening (`policies/k8s/`, `package mlsecops.k8s`)
- **Lifecycle & domain expansions:** Vector DB backup/snapshot revocation (Ch.4/Ch.7), Docker Model Runner OCI distribution/local runner guidance (`Ch.5` / `E.1.7` / `Ch.12` / `Ch.16`), `E.4` YAML↔JSON mapping, and Mermaid dark-mode `strict` rendering fixes
- **Release & CI hardening:** SHA-256-verified Conftest and `check-jsonschema` gates in `docs-check.yml`, `pip-audit` dependency checks, draft-safe `handbook-artifacts.yml` uploads, SHA-256 template verification in `scripts/build-docx.py`, and broader `SHA256SUMS` coverage
